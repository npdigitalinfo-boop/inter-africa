# 🚌 Inter Africa Bus Services

A high-performance, modern, passenger-centric web platform for **Inter Africa Bus Services**, providing seamless intercity bus ticketing, parcel logistics, real-time audio/visual delay announcements, special care protocols, and instant private charter quotations across Southern Africa.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 📢 Real-Time Delay & Road Challenge Announcements
- **Audio & Visual Broadcast**: Features speech synthesis voice announcements with custom chimes for live road and traffic advisories.
- **Smart Ticker & Noticeboard**: Highlights bus routes experiencing delays, border post queues, or roadworks with estimated impact times.

### 2. 📦 Express Bus Parcel & Package Courier
- **Send Packages on the Bus**: Direct parcel dispatch between depots with fast station-to-station tracking.
- **Instant Price Estimator**: Calculates freight charges based on weight, dimensions, and declared parcel value.

### 3. 👶 Young Passengers Protocol (Ages 5–12)
- **Safe Travel for Children**: Specially designed booking flow for young flyers/riders traveling alone or with parents.
- **Child Discount**: Automatic 20% discount applied to eligible minors.
- **Guardian Handoff Verification**: Mandatory sender and receiver guardian identification details to ensure maximum safety.

### 4. ♿ Special Care & Priority Accessibility
- **Dedicated Assistance**: Tailored services for seniors (65+), expectant mothers, and travelers requiring wheelchair or medical aid.
- **Priority Seating**: Auto-reserves ergonomic front-row accessible seats (Rows 1–2).
- **Luggage & Boarding Support**: Pre-alerts station dispatch and hostesses for personalized boarding assistance.

### 5. 🚐 Instant Private Charter & Bus Hire Proposal Engine
- **Transparent Quotation**: Instant estimation answering the four main customer questions: *Bus Type*, *Capacity*, *All-Inclusive Fare*, and *Amenities*.
- **Vehicle Fleet Options**:
  - 17-Seater Executive Sprinter
  - 35-Seater Semi-Luxury Coach
  - 65-Seater Grand Cruiser
- **Trust & Amenities**: Highlights Wi-Fi, air conditioning, GPS live tracking, driver allowance, and road transit insurance.
- **Pro-Forma Invoice Generation**: Ready-to-print quotation breakdown for corporate, school, or church trips.

### 6. 💺 Interactive Seat Selection & Ticket Booking
- Visual coach deck layout with real-time seat status (Available, Selected, Reserved, Priority Accessible).
- Instant digital ticket generation with QR verification.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Flexbox/Grid, Glassmorphism, CSS Custom Properties), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
- **Speech API**: Web SpeechSynthesis API for automated audio station announcements.
- **Media**: Optimized HD background hero video and asset illustrations.
- **Styling**: Fully responsive mobile-first architecture.
